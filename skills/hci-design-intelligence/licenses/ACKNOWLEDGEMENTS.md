# Acknowledgements and influences

## Google PAIR

This skill's approach to AI product discovery, reliance, control, and failure planning is informed by Google's [People + AI Guidebook](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/) (PAIR), published 2019 and updated 2021.

This version of the skill contains no text, worksheets, patterns, or case studies reproduced or adapted from PAIR. The files `ai-product-discovery.md`, `ai-product-workshop-templates.md`, `ai-interaction-pattern-index.md`, and `ai-facilitation-and-decision-canvas.md` were newly written for this skill. They cite and link to PAIR as recommended reading and fall under the repository's default license for original material (CC BY 4.0).

Version 0.1.0 contained files adapted from PAIR under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Those files were removed and are not part of this release.

Google does not endorse this skill.
