# Source provenance

## Epistemic roles

| Source family | Role | Do not treat as |
|---|---|---|
| W3C/WCAG and other standards | Normative requirements within declared scope | Complete proof of usability or inclusion |
| Foundational and peer-reviewed HCI | Theory, mechanisms, and empirical evidence | Universal prediction outside its conditions |
| Microsoft HAX | Human–AI interaction guidance and failure patterns | A substitute for contextual research or safety engineering |
| Google PAIR | Influence and recommended reading for AI-product questions and patterns (linked, not adapted) | A universal standard or reusable text |
| Nielsen Norman Group | Applied usability guidance and heuristics | Formal standards or direct evidence about a specific product |
| Laws of UX | Accessible reminders of psychology-related principles | Laws that guarantee an outcome |
| Direct research and operations data | Context-specific evidence | Automatically generalizable truth |
| Synthesized skill guidance | Decision support and routing | Independent empirical evidence |

## Repository policy

Every consequential claim should make its source type clear. References retain links and attribution to original material. No PAIR text is reproduced or adapted; PAIR is acknowledged in `licenses/ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md`. HAX Playbook and Designer Skills Pack-derived material retain their MIT notices.

The expanded primary-source library is a citation-and-routing bibliography. It links to standards, institutional copies, official guidance, author manuscripts, and stable scholarly records; it does not redistribute the linked works. Access status and publication details should be rechecked before release updates or verbatim quotation.

Private course files and course-derived notes are intentionally absent. No UI/UX Pro Max content or runtime is bundled.

Portable workflow concepts for design exploration, preference learning, implementation critique, mobile realism, accessibility checks, and verified visual feedback were synthesized from user-supplied skill examples. Their tool-specific instructions and code were not copied into the core; named tools remain optional adapters.
